Submission Guidelines

To maintain our reputation as a premier sports press repository, we uphold strict aesthetic and technical criteria:

  • Assets must be uploaded in uncompressed JPEG format with full embedded IPTC metadata (captions, event dates, credits).
  • Minimum capture resolution of 12 Megapixels. Crisp focus and professional exposure balance are non-negotiable.
  • Absolutely no post-process watermarks, signatures, or severe artificial graphic transformations on editorial content.
