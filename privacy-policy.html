Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At IPSPRESS, accessible from www.ipspress.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our professional partners and visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we handle it within our media distribution framework.

1. Information We Collect

When you apply for a premium account or request custom photo coverage, we collect business-related metrics strictly required for legal licensing and distribution. This includes your name, media organization, business email address, and billing information.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected parameters to maintain your archive portal credentials, deliver instant asset download logs, prevent unauthorized link scraping, and issue clean fiscal invoices for purchased copyright licenses.

3. Security of Media Assets

IPSPRESS secures all customer data using encrypted server layers. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your email records or organizational data to any third-party marketing brokers.
