Licensing Types

Last updated: May 2026

All visual assets produced and distributed by IPSPRESS operate under clear commercial standard frameworks. We offer two major categories of rights depending on your platform's scope.

1. Editorial Use Only

Assets marked for editorial deployment can be utilized exclusively within informative, descriptive news articles, blogs, and broadcast reports. They cannot be used for merchandise or paid corporate branding.

2. Commercial & Club Use

Allows sports clubs, federations, and direct marketing entities to utilize covered photography across paid promotional social campaigns, official team catalogs, and billboards.
